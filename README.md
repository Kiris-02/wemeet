# WEMEET — Fair Group Meetup & Midpoint Decision App

> 🌐 **Live Cloud Deployment**: [https://wemeet-w6j3.onrender.com](https://wemeet-w6j3.onrender.com)  
> 📦 **GitHub Repository**: [https://github.com/Kiris-02/wemeet](https://github.com/Kiris-02/wemeet)

WEMEET is a mobile-first web application designed for friends in Ho Chi Minh City to find a fair, equal-convenience meetup spot based on starting districts, travel modes, structured vibe/budget preferences, and collaborative voting.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Location Setup**:
   - Select District & Ward in HCMC.
   - Enter specific street address or use simulated GPS geolocation.
   - Choose preferred travel mode (🛵 Motorbike, 🚗 Car, 🚌 Bus, 🚶 Walk).

2. **Group Lobby & Midpoint Math**:
   - Dynamic centroid calculation across all active group members.
   - Add/edit/remove friends with custom district and transport mode.
   - Real-time midpoint district identification (e.g., District 1).

3. **Structured Vibe & Preference Filters**:
   - **Category**: Coffee & Tea, Food & Meals, Desserts & Sweets, Mall / Complex.
   - **Primary Purpose**: Work / Study, Photo Check-in, Date / Cozy, Boardgames / Fun.
   - **Budget Tier**: <50k, 50-70k, 70-100k, >100k VND.

4. **Top Matches & Per-Member Commute Matrix**:
   - Ranked candidate venues (e.g. Maison Marou Chocolate, Chè Hà Tôn Quyền, Bình Tây Traditional Bakery).
   - Shows individual travel time breakdown for each friend based on distance & transport mode.
   - View authentic menu items with pricing and descriptions.

5. **Turn-Based Collaborative Voting**:
   - **Pass & Vote**: Friends take turns selecting their venue on the phone.
   - **Auto Simulation**: 1-click simulation for quick testing.

6. **Winner Decision & Celebration**:
   - Golden trophy with confetti celebration.
   - Vote count, district area, and fairness index.
   - One-click Google Maps integration.

7. **Support & Service Terms**:
   - 24/7 AI Concierge chat.
   - Vietnamese E-commerce compliant Terms & Conditions (Luật Giao dịch điện tử số 20/2023/QH15).

8. **Interactive 10-Screen Demo Navigator**:
   - Direct jump buttons to inspect any of the 10 screens instantly.
   - Toggle between iPhone 16 Pro bezel frame and full responsive view.
